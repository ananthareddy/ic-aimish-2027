Call for Papers

We invite original, unpublished research papers focusing on the intersection of Artificial Intelligence and Dermatological Imaging.

  • Deep Learning for Medical Imaging
  • Computer Vision in Medical Imaging
  • Explainable AI (XAI) for Medical Diagnostics
  • Federated Learning for Privacy-Preserving Medical Data
  • Generative AI (GANs/Diffusion Models) for Medical Image Synthesis
  • Tele-dermatology and AI-driven Remote Diagnostics
